**Decided, 26 November 2026, all to go into `DESIGN.md`, the tokens and `app/design.tsx` with the rest of #51's answers:**

1. **The palette: C**, the user's pine-tinted neutrals with their emerald as `within`, Trim's amber and red kept. `ink-dim` was darkened from `#8C9C95` to `#5F6E68` (light) and from `#64756E` to `#8D9E97` (dark) to pass 4.5:1. Chosen over A (Trim's warm greys, teal), B (pine, a teal tuned to it) and D (Health's greys, teal), though `DESIGN.md` chose teal over green as the pair most often confused with red: red stays the darkest of the three hues in dark, and every verdict is also words. The values are in `prototype-palettes.ts` at the root.
2. **The system draws a page's title**, after the user compared it with Health on the iPhone: on iOS the large title in B612, clear over the page and collapsing into a glass bar as it scrolls; on Android the small top app bar (react-native-screens has no Material large one). Each tab gets a stack for its header. A form's way out is *Cancel* on iOS and a close X on Android. iOS ties the large title to the scroll view only when that is the screen's first view. Open: before a scroll the large title stands a bar's height lower than Health's.
3. **A list's rows stand in one rounded card** (D39), under the section's heading, as Health's sections. The card's padding narrows a row, so a figure drops below its name sooner (*about 650 kcal* does at the default size): `min-w-name` may need to come down.
4. **A form's button stays at its foot**, not in the header as iOS's *Add* and Material's *Save* are: within the thumb's reach, never under the keyboard, the form's fault just above it, and named for what it does (*Log estimated entry*), which a bar's one word cannot be.
5. **A form's fields stand in one card, a field at fault in red and a valid one in green**, the fault's words above the field (Q6, chosen over words alone, an ink outline and sign above or below, and a card per field). The user's reading: the thesis is about a day against its goals, and a form's validation is not that, so `DESIGN.md`'s "a failure takes no hue" goes for a field's fault. A fault has a warning sign and a valid field a tick, so the colour is never alone. They take their own tokens (`fault`, `valid`), drawn now in `over`'s red and `within`'s green, so a field and a day can part later. Words above, though Material and iOS put them below: the form keeps the field at fault in view when it scrolls to it, and words under it can lie behind the keyboard. A field is judged once it is left, then again as it is typed, so a red one turns green the moment it is fixed (React Hook Form's `mode: 'onTouched'`, chosen over its default `onSubmit`, under which a tick would show only beside a red after a refused save). The button still judges the whole form and brings the first fault into view.
6. **Every form opens the same way, the platform's own** (D28): iOS's page sheet (`presentation: 'modal'`), full height, scrolling, the page behind just seen at its top, and a page rising over the last on Android, Material's full-screen dialog. It replaces epic 1's mix, the grams and the estimate pushed as pages and Add food a full-screen modal. `DESIGN.md`'s "not a sheet" was about a sheet the height of its contents, which this is not. A sheet is swiped down to go, so the discard question has to catch the swipe. In the prototype it is `form.tsx`, opened by *Form* through `app/prototype/_layout.tsx`.
7. **A form's way out is an X on both platforms** (Q10): on iOS the system's own round glass X (`unstable_headerLeftItems`, an `xmark` heard as *Cancel*), as Apple's sheets have it since iOS 26; on Android Material's close X. Chosen over our *Cancel*, which iOS 26 wraps in a glass pill that does not fit it, and the system's *Cancel* in words. Kept although iOS's sheet can be swiped away: a swipe is unseen, out of reach for VoiceOver and Switch Control, and refused once something is typed.
8. **A row is deleted by a quiet bin, and by a swipe to the left beside it** (Q9, D21/D36): the bin in `ink-mid` (SF Symbols' `trash`, Material's `delete`), heard as *Delete <name>*, a full target, so it stands back from the figures; the swipe shows *Delete* in red, as iOS's own lists, the quicker way for whoever knows it. Chosen over the word, a bin in ink, and the quiet bin alone. Both ask the system's question as now. The red is a third use of it, beside a field's fault and a day over budget. The swiped button is to reach the card's rounded edge, not stop at its padding as in the prototype.
9. **A secondary button stays outlined in ink, and every button shows the platform's press** (Q11): iOS dims it, Android ripples. Chosen over a tonal secondary, which `surface-lift` on the ground barely tells from the page in light (short of the 3:1 a control's edge needs), and over today's press that shows nothing.
10. **Margins and body follow the platform guides** (Q12, #51's comment of 25 November, items 1, 2 and 6): margins of 16 on an 8 grid, through spacing tokens in px as the touch target is, since NativeWind's rem is 14 and `px-4` measured 14.1dp; body 17 on iOS and 16 on Android; a two-line row at least 72dp on Android (Material's figure, to be checked before it is written in). Not prototyped: corrections to the guides, barely seen side by side.
11. **An empty list under a summary is one line in the list's own card**, its way on at the right (*Log a weighed food, or an estimate.* · *Log*) (Q13): the summary above (the ring's empty track and *Nothing logged yet*) already says the day is empty, so the line does not say it again, and the page keeps its shape. **A page with nothing else on it** (an empty Foods, Log with no foods) takes the quiet empty state instead: centred, a sign in `ink-mid`, a bold line, a detail and an outlined button, as Health's *No Data* and Material's empty state. Chosen over epic 1's card with a black button, which said *Nothing logged yet* twice beside the ring.
12. **A failure outside a field takes a field fault's red, with the warning sign** (Q14): a page that cannot be read (*Couldn't load today's summary*, with an outlined *Retry*), a delete that failed or was refused (said above its row), and a form's *Not saved…* above its button. Chosen over epic 1's bold ink, which would read as less than a field's fault. Red with the sign means one thing everywhere: something needs you. Still words, never a toast, and the form stays open and filled.
13. **What worked on another page is told in a card with a green tick** (Q15): *Logged: Rolled oats* and its figures, the tick a valid field has, so green with a tick is *done* as red with a warning is *needs you*. Chosen over epic 1's card alone and a line with no card. It goes as `DESIGN.md` has it: when the next entry replaces it or Log is left, never on a timer.
14. **The rest of a long day is asked for by the card's own last row** (Q16): *Show all 5* in bold ink with a chevron in `ink-mid`, after a hairline, turning to *Show less*, as Health's *Show All Data*. Chosen over epic 1's outlined button under the card: the row belongs to the list it opens, and the page keeps one full-width button.

**How the decisions are held, after the grilling** (the user, 26 November 2026: as easy as possible for a later session to follow). Cheapest to follow first:

1. **Components, so the right thing is the default**: one form card with its fields and their `fault` and `valid` states, judged `onTouched`; one list card with the quiet bin and the swipe; one way to present a form, with its native X; one button with the platform's press; the header options in one place. A screen gets a decision wrong only by leaving its component.
2. **Lint rules for what can be checked**, beside the `design/*` and `forms/*` rules: a form's `useForm` sets `mode: 'onTouched'`; a form screen uses the shared presentation and its X; `fault` and `valid` only on a field; no bare `Pressable` outside `src/components`.
3. **Skills and docs**: `DESIGN.md` gives each decision and its reason, `app/design.tsx` shows each live, and `platform-conventions` and `rn-dev` point at the components and name the rule that holds each.

**Teardown:** `app/prototype/colours.tsx`, `form.tsx`, `_layout.tsx`, `prototype-palettes.ts`, this section, the link in `app/design.tsx` (and its `Link` import), then `exempt.sh off` when `app/prototype/` is empty.
